# Research design, analysis, and revision

## Original research

Recover the actual study records before drafting methods or results. If none exist, produce a protocol or proposal and a manuscript skeleton with results pending. A polished plan is not completed research.

Connect the research question to outcomes, population or dataset, comparison or baseline, and analysis. Document sampling, exclusions, preprocessing, missing-data treatment, assumptions, and reproducibility details. Explain exploratory versus prespecified analyses; do not retrospectively label an analysis preregistered.

When executing analysis, retain data/code provenance, software versions, seeds where relevant, and a record of transformations. Use methods justified by the design. Report uncertainty and effect sizes where applicable; do not infer causality from association or equate lack of statistical significance with no effect. Report checks actually performed and separate unexecuted recommendations.

For computational papers, document data splits, leakage checks, comparable baselines, metrics, ablations where warranted, and compute/configuration details. Do not present proposed benchmarks as measured performance.

Use actual ethics approval or consent information provided by the author. If human-subject procedures are missing, identify the gap and verify relevant institutional requirements before proposing collection. Do not claim approval, consent, or compliance without records.

## Reviews

Distinguish narrative, scoping, and systematic reviews. Preserve the user's chosen type. A systematic review needs a documented question/protocol, search strategy and dates, eligibility criteria, screening records, extraction process, and appropriate appraisal; align reporting with the field's current guidance.

Record exclusion reasons and derive counts from actual screening records. Do not invent flow-diagram counts or imply independent dual screening when only one reviewer screened. A preliminary synthesis with incomplete coverage remains preliminary. Perform meta-analysis only when the outcomes, designs, and available estimates justify combining studies.

## Revision and reviewer response

Read the manuscript and all comments. Create a matrix with comment ID, requested change, action taken, manuscript location, response, and unresolved issue. Prioritize validity and missing evidence before stylistic polish.

Make supported edits and describe exactly what changed. For disagreement, give a respectful evidence-based explanation; do not promise analyses or experiments that were not performed. Check that abstract, tables, discussion, and conclusion reflect revised findings.

Before submission preparation, verify current venue instructions including article type, length, citation style, anonymization, file requirements, declarations, and applicable AI-use disclosure. Prepare drafts of declarations only from known facts. Provide a readiness report separating completed checks, unresolved author input, and requirements that could not be verified. Do not submit or contact editors/reviewers without explicit authorization.
