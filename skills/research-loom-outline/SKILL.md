# Paper Outline

Use the agreed brief, evidence synthesis, and actual methods/results where available. Choose structure by paper type, discipline, and current venue requirements. Link each section's argument to supporting claims and evidence. Identify the contribution, limitations, figures/tables, and remaining inputs.

For missing original-research data, outline a protocol or mark results pending; do not silently turn it into a different article type. Multiple goals can become one paper only when the author agrees that they form a coherent contribution. Otherwise propose separate outlines.

Deliver a section outline with purpose, evidence/claim references, planned figures, dependencies, and drafting order. Include unresolved author decisions and a realistic readiness assessment.
